    • The Baltic Olympiad in Informatics 2026 will be organized in Finland during April 15–19, 2026. The contest will take place in the Helsinki metropolitan area, at the Aalto University campus in Otaniemi, Espoo.

    The following countries have been invited: Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Germany, Iceland, Latvia, Lithuania, Norway, Poland, Sweden, Ukraine

    Accommodation is provided at Hotel Heymo 1, which is within walking distance from the contest venue.

    • April 16 (Thursday): Contest day 1

    • April 18 (Saturday): Excursion, Closing ceremony
    • April 19 (Sunday): Departures
